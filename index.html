Hi.

I was a PhD student studying Computer Science at University of Chicago, advised by Sanjay Krishnan as a part of the ChiData Group. I did my undergraduate at Princeton University, and am originally from Ottawa, Canada.

My research was focused on building systems for semi-structured machine learning data. My dissertation project was working on the TableVault system. TableVault aimed to answer: how do we systematically query from ML-generated data across distributed AI experiments?. Other research projects included Jupyter Notebook tracking (Github), data lineage and provenance (Github), and machine learning error classification (Github).

As of June 2026, I have joined Google as a part of the Cloud AI infrastructure team working on metrics over TPUs (Tensor Processing Units).
